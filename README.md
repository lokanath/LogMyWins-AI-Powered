# LogMyWins — AI-Powered Achievement Journal

A private, AI-powered wins journal for business professionals. Log each achievement with its date, description, and business impact, then generate polished, executive-grade achievement summaries with AI — ready to paste into reviews, portfolios, and performance conversations.

## What it does

- **Log wins** — Capture a professional achievement in three fields: date, what you did, and the business impact it created. Entries are private to your account.
- **Chronological feed** — Every win is listed in a clean, date-stamped journal you can scan at a glance. Edit or delete any entry inline.
- **AI achievement summaries** — Pick a time frame (last month, last quarter, or full year) and generate 3–5 concise, business-language bullet points that turn your logged wins into a polished accomplishment statement. Copy to clipboard in one click.
- **Private & secure** — Each user's journal is isolated with row-level security; nobody sees your wins but you.
- **Sign in your way** — Email/password or Google sign-in.
- **Thoughtful guardrails** — Future dates are blocked, empty fields are validated, and the AI never invents facts beyond your own entries.
